# Generated backdrop prompts

Paste-ready. Written to the [08](../docs/08-brand.md) palette, not the reference account's warm brocade. See [10](../docs/10-imagery-rights.md) for why the piece is never generated and how to composite it.

**Two rules that are not stylistic:**
1. **Bare ear, no jewellery.** The earring is a real photograph, dropped in afterwards. Generating one and painting it out leaves artefacts.
2. **No named artist, no named photographer, no real person.** Prompting toward a specific protected work can reproduce it, which puts you back where you started.

---

## A · The painted sitter

```
Oil portrait of an East Asian woman, turned three-quarters away from the
viewer, head angled so that one ear is fully visible, unobstructed and
completely bare. Dark hair gathered up off the neck. Nothing worn on the
ear, neck or hair — no jewellery of any kind.

Painted in a nineteenth-century academic manner: visible brushwork, the
tooth of the canvas showing through thin passages, soft blended
transitions in the skin, a slightly cracked varnish sheen.

Cool restrained palette — grey-green and pale silver-grey ground, deep
indigo in the shadows, the warm skin the only warmth in the frame.
Soft directional light from a high north window, falling across the
cheekbone and the side of the neck. Plain dark neutral background,
shallow depth, generous empty space to one side of the head.

Calm, unsmiling, self-possessed. Muted, quiet, unsentimental.
4:5 portrait.
```

**Avoid:** earrings, necklace, hair ornament, gold, jewellery, warm brown tones, sepia, brocade, damask, gilt frame, chandelier, flowers, text, watermark, signature, modern makeup, glamour lighting, high saturation.

---

## B · The old photograph

```
Black and white photograph of an East Asian woman in strict profile,
facing left. Dark hair pulled back tightly into a low knot, the ear
fully exposed, unobstructed and completely bare. Nothing worn on the
ear, neck or hair — no jewellery of any kind.

Shot on medium format film: heavy silver-halide grain, deep blacks, a
clean roll-off in the highlights, shallow depth of field with the ear
and jawline sharp. Hard directional light from the side, raking across
the cheekbone, one edge of the face falling into shadow.

Plain pale grey seamless background, no texture, no props.
Still, composed, unsmiling. Editorial rather than commercial.
4:5 portrait.
```

**Avoid:** earrings, necklace, jewellery, colour, sepia, toning, smiling, soft focus glow, beauty retouching, ring light, text, watermark, logo, border, film sprockets, date stamp.

## Adjustments worth trying

| If | Change |
|---|---|
| Ear is obscured by hair | Add `hair tucked firmly behind the ear, ear completely clear of hair` |
| A jewellery item appears anyway | Move `no jewellery` to the front of the prompt, repeat it once at the end |
| Too warm / too gilded | Add `cool neutral colour temperature, no gold, no warm brown` |
| Face reads as generated | Add `natural skin texture, visible pores, slight asymmetry, no retouching` |
| Not enough room for the piece | Add `ear positioned right of centre with empty space around it` |
| Grain too clean (register B) | Add `pronounced film grain, slightly uneven, visible in the mid-tones` |

**Generate several and keep the ones whose light direction you can actually match on a shoot day.** That constraint eliminates more candidates than aesthetics does, and it is the one that decides whether the composite reads as real ([10](../docs/10-imagery-rights.md)).
