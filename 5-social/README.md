## Where it stands · 13 Oct 2026

**The strategy is written, but no accounts exist.**

- **The rhythm:** a weekly **Edition** at a fixed day and hour. The day hasn't been chosen ([#22](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)).
  - Monday: teaser.
  - Wednesday: authentication or styling.
  - Thursday: Edition email at 6pm, then the Edition goes live at 7pm.
  - Friday: what's left.
- **Channels:**
  - **Instagram** is primary.
  - **Email** is where the money is.
  - **Pinterest** is cheap and evergreen.
  - **TikTok** is for discovery only.
  - **小红书** is for UK and EU Chinese-speaking buyers and for search, not mainland China ([#76](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)).
- **The Instagram grid** ([D19](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md), *proposed*):
  - Three posts per Edition, each frame type in its own column.
  - The condition frame never goes on the grid.
  - *Instagram is where people fall for a piece; the product page is where we prove it.*
- **Atmosphere comes from the Japan sourcing,** not from borrowed paintings ([05](05-social.md) update ii, [D20](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)). Generated backdrops are allowed; composites onto other people's images are not.
- **A broadcast channel** is the free forerunner to the email list. Start it from week one.
- **Paid ads come later** ([D11](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)): only once the Edition sells organically, and optimised for list sign-ups. The starting budget is about £450–600 a month ([14](14-marketing.md)).

**Blocked by the name.** `@hikari` is taken ([#64](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)), and the name itself is in question ([#57](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)). Handles can't be checked by bot, because Instagram redirects automated requests ([#59](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)).
