# Hikari — 光

An independent resale house for vintage designer jewellery, sourced in **Japan** and sold into the **UK**.

- **Product:** vintage and archival costume jewellery: Christian Dior, Chanel, Givenchy, YSL, Nina Ricci, Grosse, and the better mid-century American makers.
- **Price band:** £100–300 retail.
- **Supply:** Japan (auctions, reuse chains and wholesale channels).
- **Demand:** the United Kingdom first.
- **Stock model:** one of one. Every piece is a single item.

**Status · 13 Oct 2026.** The plan and the Shopify theme are built. There are no real products, no accounts and no sales yet. The name is under a trade mark question ([#57](0-plan/99-open-questions.md)). Nothing has been tested with a real customer ([07](0-plan/07-roadmap.md), Phase 0).
