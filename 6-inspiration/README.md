## The rule: copy how they do it, never their images

Already worked out on the first reference ([05](../5-social/05-social.md), [D20](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)):

| Take | Never take |
|---|---|
| Structure: grid rhythm, how much of the feed is mood versus product, how Stories are sequenced | **Their photographs**, even reposted with credit, cropped or AI-edited |
| Formats: authentication Reels, category Highlights, broadcast channels | **Composites** of a piece onto a painting or photograph someone else made |
| How their copy is built: what they say first, which facts they give | **Their words**, captions and bios |
| Things they show the business needs: a bridal line, gift framing | **Their logo, lettering or brand identity** — or the fashion houses' |
| | **Their growth method.** Follow-for-follow inflates numbers, not sales. |

Two working habits follow from that:

- **Screenshots go in a private Drive folder, never in this repo.** The repo is public, so committing someone's photo republishes it.
- **A reference that changes the *frame* rather than the subject is a reversal of [D2](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md), not a refinement.** The frame is grounds, palette and type; the subject is the piece itself. A frame change needs a decision, not a mood board.
