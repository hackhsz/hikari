## What applying depends on

- **Stock in hand.** Farnham asks what you sell. A stall needs at least ~15 pieces to look like a stall. How many pieces exist today?
- **An Instagram presence.** Farnham asks for an established one. Handles wait on the name ([#57](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md), [#59](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)), so send photographs of the pieces instead ([Thread 2](../2-photos/README.md)).
- **The name on the stall.** Use printed signage only, nothing made, until #57 clears.
- **Era rules.** Farnham is pre-1990 only and Lou Lou's is 1920s–80s only. 1990s pieces stay home for those two.

## Stall kit

- [ ] Card reader: Shopify POS, which [Thread 1](../1-shop/README.md) sets up. **No bank transfers at a stall** ([D4](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)).
- [ ] A **sign-up QR code** for the Edition list. Collecting addresses is the stall's main job.
- [ ] A card for each piece: house, year, grade, price. Wording from [Thread 3](../3-copy/README.md).
- [ ] A grade card explaining I–IV.
- [ ] A mirror, trays and the paper ground. Make the display look like the photographs.
- [ ] A float, bags and a printed returns card.

## After each market

Write five lines here:
- what sold, and at what price
- what people asked before buying
- how many sign-ups
- the cost, all in
- would we go back?

Over a few markets, the questions people ask become a list of what the copy and the product page don't yet answer.
