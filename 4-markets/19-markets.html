Markets, October to December

Where Hikari can take a stall between 4 October and 6 December 2026. The rule was fairs led by clothing and jewellery, whose buyers will pay £140–160 for a piece. Antiques fairs with furniture are out, and so are flea, kilo and thrift sales.

Researched 4 Oct 2026 London first, plus Brighton, Bristol, Bath, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Edinburgh and day trips from London About 70 events checked

The short answer

The two London fairs whose buyers match Hikari, Frock Me! and A Current Affair, are closed to new traders this season. Two good ones can still be booked: Vintage Fashion Fair Farnham and The Good Clothes Show in Manchester. A Friday at Portobello Green is the cheap way to test a stall first.

Farnham is the only fair within reach that sells nothing but vintage fashion. The Good Clothes Show is the only one with paid entry and a stand sized for jewellery. Nothing in Brighton, Bristol, Leeds, Glasgow or Edinburgh fits: what runs there is flea, thrift, kilo, or antiques with furniture. There is no fair anywhere in the UK just for vintage costume jewellery that isn't a traditional antiques fair.

Vintage Fashion Fair Farnham

Strong · book

Farnham Maltings, Bridge Square, Farnham GU9 7QR · about an hour from London, next to Guildford

Sat 31 Oct  ·  Sat 28 Nov 10:00–16:00 organiser's site

What's sold
"Strictly an authentic vintage fashion only fair": clothing, accessories and textiles. Everything must be made before 1990. No crafts, homeware, reproductions or "vintage style". 27 hand-picked stalls. It runs every two months and started in 2016.
Crowd
£4 entry. The venue describes the range as "from budget-friendly items to premium designer pieces".
Stall
About 3m × 2m: two rails plus a table, with chairs, extra tables and one parking space included. About £95, a figure from a third-party listing; the organiser doesn't publish its fee.
Apply
Email fair@vintagefashionfairfarnham.co.uk with who you are, your stock, where you sell now, and links to your website and socials. No deadline is published.
Break-even
2 pieces

Watch: they ask for "an established Instagram presence", and Hikari's handles aren't settled yet (99 #59). Send photographs of ten pieces instead. Pieces from the 1990s can't be sold here.

The Good Clothes Show · Manchester

Strong · book

Manchester Central · a target city for the core customer (doc 03)

Sat 7 – Sun 8 Nov Sat 10–18 · Sun 11–17 organiser's site

What's sold
"The largest UK exhibition of independent brands, artisans, vintage and preloved retailers and ethical startups." There's also a catwalk, talks and a Christmas market. Vintage sellers share the floor with new ethical brands; it isn't vintage-only. Past exhibitors include several vintage dealers and a vintage brooch seller.
Crowd
Paid entry: £20 on Saturday, £17.50 on Sunday (students £7). The best filter for serious buyers of anything on this page, with a sustainability-minded crowd.
Stand
2m × 1m, £200 + VAT: "Recommended for jewellery and smaller items". It comes with one table (no rail), a chair, name branding and 2 exhibitor passes. Hikari isn't VAT-registered, so the VAT is a real cost: £240.
Apply
Exhibitor enquiries · registration form. No deadline is published. It's five weeks away.
Break-even
4 pieces for the stand, before travel and a hotel night

Watch: two days of trading plus travel from London. This is the only stand on the page that gets a jewellery seller in front of a paying fashion crowd in a target city before Christmas.

Portobello Green Market · Fridays

Test · walk-up

Under the Westway canopy, Portobello Road, W10

Every Friday: 9, 16, 23, 30 Oct · 6, 13, 20, 27 Nov · 4 Dec weekly pattern, inferred

What's sold
Friday is "Vintage & Antiques traders only", and fashion-led. Stylists and trend-setters shop it, but so do bargain hunters, and there's plenty of costume jewellery already. It's outdoors.
Pitch
£30 on Fridays (£50 on Saturdays). No pre-booking. Arrive from 6:30am at the Westway Trust, 1 Thorpe Close W10 5XL. Pitches go first come, first served after 8:30am, and a place isn't guaranteed.
Contact
Pgm-enquiries@everydayurbanism.co.uk
Break-even
1 piece

Use it for: a rehearsal before Farnham. Practise the display, find out how people react to £150, and note every question asked before someone buys.

Lou Lou's Vintage Fair · London & Manchester

Possible

Corner Corner, Canada Water SE16 7LL · Victoria Baths, Manchester M13 0FE

Sun 15 Nov (London)  ·  Sun 22 Nov (Manchester) 11:00–16:00 organiser's site

What's sold
Stock from the 1920s to the 1980s: women's wear, menswear, jewellery, collectables and some homeware. Jewellery traders are invited.
Crowd
£3 entry. It promotes finds "at an affordable price", so Hikari would be the most expensive stall in the room.
Apply
Apply separately for each event on Eventaly (London) or Eventaly (Manchester). They reply within 10 working days. Fees show only after a free login. trade@loulousvintage.com

Watch: 15 Nov is the same day as Frock Me! in Victoria, which is the better day to spend walking the room as a visitor. Nothing from the 1990s.

Old Spitalfields Market

Possible

16 Horner Square, E1 6EW

Daily (not Thursdays) · Thursday Antiques & Vintage, 8:00–17:00 weekly; Christmas switch-on Wed 11 Nov ✓

What's sold
The Daily Market has 70+ fashion, homeware and art stalls. On Thursdays, 80+ stalls sell "vintage art, maps, furniture, home-wares, jewellery and fashion": jewellery is strong there, but it shares the day with furniture.
Crowd
Tourists, City workers and East Londoners, with collectors and stylists on Thursdays. A lot of people, but not a premium crowd.
Pitch
Daily: register on the trader portal, then book in the app. Fees aren't published. Thursday: £40 / £45 / £60 by size, via Sherman & Waterman (third-party and undated).

Don't confuse it with the Spitalfields Traders Market in Crispin Place, run by a different operator. Its own FAQ says it is not accepting new jewellery applications because the category is oversubscribed.

Frock Me! Vintage Fashion Fair

Waiting list

Running since 1997. Designers, stylists and costume departments buy here. £8 entry.

Sun 18 Oct, Kensington Town Hall · Sun 15 Nov, RHS Lindley Hall, Victoria · Sun 6 Dec, Chelsea Old Town Hall 11:00–17:30 organiser's site

What's sold
Vintage clothing, jewellery and textiles, from Victorian pieces to pre-loved designer labels. Highly curated, with no furniture. The Victoria fair is billed with "vintage jewellery dealers". This is the closest match to Hikari in the country.
Status
"Currently fully-booked… a substantial waiting list for stands." "Applications on hold." New stands open only "when a current exhibitor bows out (which is very rare)".
Contact
bookings@frockmevintagefashion.com · 020 7503 9171. They "always welcome meeting prospective applicants at the fair."

Do: join the waiting list this week, then walk the 18 Oct fair and introduce yourself. Seeing what the jewellery dealers there charge also checks the £140–160 pricing.

What to send, in order

  1. Email Farnham for 31 Oct and 28 Nov. The first fair is four weeks away. With no Instagram yet, attach photographs of ten pieces and a line on the Japan sourcing.
  2. Enquire with The Good Clothes Show about a 2m × 1m stand. It's five weeks away. Ask whether any other vintage jewellery sellers are booked.
  3. Email Frock Me! to join the waiting list, and ask to hear about cancellations for 15 Nov and 6 Dec. Put 18 Oct in the diary as a visit.
  4. Fill in A Current Affair's exhibitor form, aiming at the next London edition.
  5. Optional: do one Friday at Portobello Green before 31 Oct, as a rehearsal.

Era rules

Farnham accepts only pre-1990 stock, and Lou Lou's only 1920s–1980s. Leave 1990s Dior and Chanel at home for those two. The Good Clothes Show, Portobello Green and Frock Me! have no era cut-off.

It fits the plan

Doc 14 (item 5) already treats a stall as list-building, not a breach of D3. So the stall's main job is collecting email addresses for Edition night. Take card payments; a bank transfer at a stall undoes the trust work (D4).

The name

A stall puts "Hikari" in front of the public while the Class 14 question (99 #57) is still open. Keep signage cheap and printed until it's answered, not a made sign.
