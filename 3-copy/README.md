## The rules, on one page

**Voice** ([08](../brand/08-brand.md)):
- Short declarative sentences. Name the object, the house, the year and the condition.
- **Never** "stunning", "gorgeous", "must-have" or "obsessed". **No** exclamation marks or emoji in product copy.
- Specifics do the work adjectives can't: *"Gold-tone, crystal-set. Signed Chr. Dior on the reverse. Some loss to the plating at the clip. Found in Tokyo, March 2026."*

**Brand names are metadata, never identity** ([04](../1-shop/04-site-ia.md)):
- "Vintage Christian Dior earrings" is fine.
- **Never** "Dior at Hikari", "Official", "Authorised" or "Stockist". Never the house's logo or lettering. Never its campaign imagery.
- The trademark disclaimer goes in the footer and on every designer page. Have the wording checked first ([#82](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)).

**Metal and stones** ([04](../1-shop/04-site-ia.md), [archive/cecilia](../archive/cecilia/README.md)):
- Say **"gold-tone"** or **"gold-plated"**. Never "gold" on its own.
- Paste or crystal is never called a gemstone.

**Price and the bag** ([D12](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md), [D17](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)):
- The price shown is the price paid.
- Never write anything that suggests a piece in the bag is being held for you.

**The house words** ([08](../brand/08-brand.md)):

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Edition No. 14** | The weekly drop, numbered and dated. Never "drop". |
| **The Alcove** | The single feature piece on the homepage |
| **The Record** | Sold pieces kept live on the site |
| **Outposts** | Our marketplace listings |
| **Grade I–IV** | The condition scale |
| **Found in Tokyo, March 2026** | The provenance line |
| **The Room** | Reserved for a future physical space |

**The name may change** ([#57](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)). Keep "Hikari" out of anything expensive to redo, such as printed matter or a domain, until it clears.

## Templates (drafts — refine against the first real pieces)

**Product title:** `Vintage {House} {Stone/finish} {Type}, c.{year}`
*Vintage Christian Dior Crystal Clip Earrings, c.1987*

**Product page fields** (these match the theme's metafields):

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Piece | Crystal-set clip earrings, c.1987 |
| Grade | II |
| Condition | Some loss to the plating at the clip. All stones present. |
| Size | 42 × 31 mm · 18 g the pair |
| Mark | *Chr. Dior © Germany* on the reverse of each, which dates them to the 1980s |
| Era | 1980s |
| Provenance | Found in Tokyo, March 2026 |

**Instagram caption, one per frame posted:**
- Line 1: house, piece, year.
- Line 2: one fact about the piece or the find.
- Line 3: `Edition No. 14 · Thursday 7pm · link in bio`

No adjectives in line 1.

**小红书 cover title** ([05](../5-social/05-social.md)): `东京中古 | 1987 Dior 水钻夹式耳环 · Grade II`. The body carries the knowledge (how to read the mark) rather than selling. The voice rule holds in Chinese too.

**Edition email:**
- Subject: `Edition No. 14 — three pieces, tonight at 7`
- One line per piece, with a link. Subscribers see it an hour before the public.

## Pages the shop is waiting for

In the order they block things:

1. **Condition grades I–IV** ([#21](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)). Needed before the first listing; every piece is graded against them.
2. **How we authenticate.** Explain the method, not a badge: marks by era, construction, clasp, plating. [archive/cecilia](../archive/cecilia/README.md) has a first list of Dior marks by era; verify them against real pieces first.
3. **What we retouch, and what we never do** ([#40](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md), [D10](../0-plan/DECISIONS.md)). A trust asset nobody else in the category has.
4. **Delivery and returns:** the 14-day right to cancel, stated plainly, and who pays return postage ([#32](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)). Also "duty paid, no customs charges".
5. **About.** Depends on founder-forward vs brand-anonymous ([#8](../0-plan/99-open-questions.md)), so decide that first.
6. **Gifting:** "gifts under £150", delivery cut-off dates and the exchange route, for segment C ([03](../0-plan/03-customer.md)). **This is the Christmas page, so it's timely now.**
