# Market scout — sources

Where to look each week. Built from the first run (2026-10-13). The weekly run adds what proved productive and moves what died to the dead-ends lists. Listing sites and aggregators are **leads, not facts**: verify dates and fees on the organiser's own page.

### Search queries that worked
- "<organiser> become a trader" / "trade with us" / "exhibitors apply"
- "<market> stallholder booking 2026 pitch price"
- "Old Truman Brewery markets" leads to trumanbrewery.com/web/markets.pl (Backyard Market, Upmarket, Artisan, Brick Lane Vintage Market; no application route found yet)
- Fetching an organiser's sitemap.xml (e.g. Alfies) surfaced recruitment posts that search did not.
- Google Form og:description text gives full rules/prices (Urban Makers).

## Manchester, Bristol, Glasgow, Brighton

First run 2026-10-13. These are the pages and searches that actually gave verifiable data.

### Organiser pages (check weekly)
- **Judy's Vintage Fair** (now "Vintage Furniture & Flea"; the old judysvintagefair.co.uk and vintagefair.co.uk are dead):
  - Trader event list with booking links: https://judysvintagefair.com/traders/
  - All events: https://judysvintagefair.com/events/
  - Sitemap, which reveals Bristol, Manchester and Brighton event slugs before they are promoted: https://judysvintagefair.com/sitemap/
  - Per-event stall prices are public at `https://judysvintagefair.com/trader-bookings/?eventid=<id>`
- **Lou Lou's Vintage Fair**: https://www.loulousvintagefair.com/ (dates) and https://eventaly.com/organiser/lou-lous-vintage-market/ (applications; Manchester event is /event/lou-lous-manchester/)
- **Craft & Flea**: https://eventaly.com/organiser/craft-flea/ (Manchester, Glasgow and Brighton events; no Bristol edition) and https://www.thecraftandflea.com/pages/trader-faqs (prices)
- **Textile Society fairs**: https://www.textilesociety.org.uk/event/manchester-antique-vintage-textile-fair (book via fairs@textilesociety.org.uk)
- **Love Fairs Brighton**: https://lovefairs.com/brighton_exhibitors/ (dates and pitch prices)
- **Bristol St Nicholas Markets**: https://www.bristol.gov.uk/st-nicholas-markets/trade-with-us. Vacancies are posted on @stnicksmarket (Instagram/Facebook). The Christmas market page is …/trade-at-st-nicks-christmas-market
- **Tobacco Factory Sunday Market**: https://eventaly.com/event/tobacco-factory-sunday-market/ (dates and pitch prices)
- **JAC Fairs Glasgow**: https://www.jacfairs.com/glasgow-sell-with-us. Watch for the jewellery ban being lifted.
- **Snoopers Paradise**: https://snoopersparadise.co.uk/trade-with-us. Watch for the "closed to clothes and jewellery traders" notice being removed.
- **Brighton Etsy Team**: https://www.brightonetsyteam.com/christmas-market (the application window has historically been early to late July)
- **Brighton Open Market**: https://www.brightonopenmarket.co.uk/prices--stall-map.html
- **Brighton Flea Market (Kemptown)**: https://www.flea-markets.co.uk/
- **Afflecks**: https://afflecks.com/pages/space-to-let
- **The Barras**: https://market.barrowland.co.uk/blogs/the-barras/pop-up-permanent

### Listing and aggregator sites (leads only)
- **Eventaly** (trader platform used by Lou Lou's, Craft & Flea and Tobacco Factory). Event pages show dates and often prices without a login.
- **Antiques Atlas**: city fair lists at https://www.antiques-atlas.com/listings/greater-manchester/?type=af, /listings/bristol/?type=af and /listings/west-scotland/?type=af, plus monthly calendars at /dbevents/. This is how Vintage@The Monastery (Decorative Fairs, 07809 236 607) turned up.
- **whatsonglasgow.co.uk**: Glasgow market listings (Craft & Flea, Thrift & Flea, Merchant Square, Wasps).
- **Eventbrite**: organiser pages for ticketed fairs, used to confirm Judy's and kilo-sale dates.

### Productive search queries
- `"<city> vintage fair 2026 traders wanted"` / `"<city> vintage fair stallholders apply"`
- `"Judy's Vintage Fair" 2025 OR 2026` (this is how the rebrand turned up)
- `"<market name> trader application stall fee"` (Barras, Levenshulme, St Nicholas, Tobacco Factory)
- `"Vintage at The Monastery" Gorton` / `Decorative Fairs vintage fair Monastery`
- `Textile Society Manchester Antique and Vintage Textile Fair <year> exhibitors`
- `Brighton Etsy Christmas Market <year> sellers apply`

### Unresolved leads (worth one more check)
- **Vintage@The Monastery, Manchester** (Decorative Fairs): no current date. themonastery.co.uk returns 403 to fetches, so phone or email the organiser instead.
- **Altrincham Market**: altrinchammarket.co.uk was unreachable. Applications go to iwanttotrade@marketoperations.co.uk.
- **Stockport Vintage Village** (2nd Sunday monthly, Stockport Market Hall): tracked since run 2, but the official site timed out again, so details are unverified. Contact is info@vintagevillagestockportmarket.co.uk.
- **Levenshulme**: the original Levenshulme Market paused in 2023, and levymarket.com was down. The Levy Artisan Market (Independent Street plus the council, 2nd Sunday) is artisan-led and its rules are unverified.
- **Craft & Flea Brighton** (St Bart's, Sat 17 Oct 2026): too close to apply this cycle. Watch Eventaly for the next Brighton date.
- **Manchester City Council specialist markets / Christmas Markets**: council pages return 403 to fetches, so use search-engine extracts or a manual browser check.

### Dead ends (don't re-check)
- judysvintagefair.co.uk and vintagefair.co.uk are dead; judysvintage.com is an unavailable Shopify store.
- makersmarket.co.uk is a domain for sale (the Makers Market brand is gone).
- prelovedkilo.com does not resolve.
- bazaarbrighton.co.uk does not resolve.
- "Vintage Fashion Fair" (Frock Me!, Farnham, Wanstead) is London and the South East only, with no editions in these four cities.
- Glasgow Vintage & Flea Market: no sign of it since 2018 and the site is unreachable (run 2).
- Kinning Park Antique Centre, Glasgow: no website and no dealer-letting information (run 2).
- Merchant Square and Wasps (Glasgow): researched at run 2 and ruled out as makers-only; tracked at fit 1.
