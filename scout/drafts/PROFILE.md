## Check before every application

- **Era rules.** No piece on the site states a date. Several fairs restrict eras: Lou Lou's, for example, wants 1920s–1980s. Many of the Dior logo and oblique pieces look like 1990s–2000s designs. Only list the pieces you can date inside a fair's range.
- **Public liability insurance.** Flea London requires **£5m** cover and a certificate upload. Camden Passage requires your own insurance. Most others recommend it. Buy a market-trader policy before the first application that needs it.
- **Photos.** Product photos come from the site. A photo of a stall set-up helps: the homepage plate captioned *"At the stall, October"* may be usable if it shows the table.
- **Prior trading.** If you've already traded at a market, name it. Organisers weigh it.
- **Authenticity wording.** Say "authentic vintage" and "authenticated". Never imply a brand relationship: the site's footer disclaimer covers that ([04](../../docs/04-site-ia.md)).
