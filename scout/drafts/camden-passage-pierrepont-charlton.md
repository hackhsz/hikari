# Camden Passage: Pierrepont Arcade & Charlton Place

**What it is:** an antiques and vintage market in Angel, Islington.
- Wed & Sat: antiques
- Thursday: vintage fashion day at Pierrepont Arcade
- Fri & Sun: general antiques at Pierrepont

You get a covered, lit stall with a ~6 × 3 ft trestle table.
**Apply:** the stall enquiry form at https://www.camdenpassageislington.co.uk/market-stall-enquiry.html (the page blocks automated reading, so these fields come from run 1's notes).
**Fee:** not published.
**Profile:** [PROFILE.md](PROFILE.md)

> Vintage costume jewellery is already established here, but the existing stalls advertise "dealer friendly prices". **Thursday's vintage fashion day** is the better audience for £100–300 pieces.
