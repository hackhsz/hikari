# Old Spitalfields Market: Thursday Antiques & Vintage

**What it is:** the Thursday antiques and vintage market, 8am–5pm, with 80+ stalls. The market's own list includes jewellery and fashion. Other days are a general market.
**Apply:** register on the trader portal at https://portal.oldspitalfieldsmarket.com/uk/org-application/01d23733-2c6f-477e-a046-2677d9c1d1c2. The market approves "if we like your brand and products". After approval you book individual days in the portal or app.
**Fee:** not published. Run 1 found indicative day rates of £45–£60 on weekdays.
**Profile:** [PROFILE.md](PROFILE.md)
